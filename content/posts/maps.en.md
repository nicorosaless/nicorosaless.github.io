Respira Hackathon 2026 took place in Barcelona from October 1 to 3. One of its challenges asked to detect lung damage in people who do not yet meet the criterion for COPD, using their chest CT and clinical data. COPD is diagnosed with spirometry, when the FEV1/FVC ratio drops below 0.70, and what comes before it, pre-COPD, still has no agreed definition.

The data came from the EARLY COPD cohort: 86 current and former smokers aged 35 to 50, each with a CT scan, spirometry and clinical history. At that age, CT changes are still subtle.

Our project, MAPS, won the challenge. It measures emphysema and how much of the airway tree is visible on the CT, compares each measure with what is expected for someone of the same age, sex, height and smoking history, and flags who, without obstruction, already has a CT that looks like COPD.
